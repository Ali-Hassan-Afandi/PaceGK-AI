# PACE GK Academy — November 2026 Session and Course Details

## Document purpose
This document contains the session announcement and course details shown in the PACE GK Academy posters. It is intended as a knowledge source for the PACE GK Academy RAG chatbot.

## Academy and mentor
- Academy: Pakistan Academy for Competitive Exams (PACE) / PACE GK Academy
- Mentor: Rana Muhammad Aslam
- Mentor designation shown on the posters: GK Mentor; PPSC / FPSC Mentor
- Slogan shown on the new-session poster: Your Success, Our Mission

## November 2026 new session
- Session: New session for PPSC, FPSC and all general one-paper exams
- Starting date shown: 1 November 2026
- Duration: 3 months
- Class days: Monday to Friday
- Class timing: 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM (shown on the separate course-details poster)
- Mode: Online classes and recorded lectures
- Recording availability: Recordings are available for 24 hours only.
- Tests: Weekly and monthly tests
- Fee: Rs. 4,000 per month
- Limited seats: The poster encourages applicants to enroll and secure their place.

## Exams and posts listed for preparation
The new-session poster lists preparation for:
1. AD LDA
2. AD PESSI
3. NCCIA Jobs
4. Naib Tehsildar (BOR)
5. Intelligence Officer
6. Inspector Labour Department
7. Assistant (Various Departments)

The poster also describes the session as preparation for PPSC, FPSC and all general one-paper exams.

## Course structure and subjects
The course-details poster describes a 3-month session covering one paper and 10 subjects.

The 10 subjects listed are:
1. English
2. General Knowledge
3. Political Map / Physical Map of the World
4. Pak Affairs
5. Indo Pak History
6. Current Affairs
7. Islamiat and Islamic History
8. Everyday Science
9. Computer
10. Maths / General Ability
11. Urdu

Note: The poster says “10 subjects” but its printed list contains 11 numbered entries because Pak Affairs and Indo Pak History appear together in one numbered entry. Preserve the poster's wording; do not silently remove a subject.
